Privacy Policy

Last updated: 11 November 2026

1. Who we are

Turing is a personal tool operated by Lazar Stojanovic (lazarst.work@gmail.com).

2. Data we access

When the operator authorizes the app with TikTok Login, Turing receives an access token and the basic profile of that single account (open ID, display name). It uses this only to upload video drafts to that account.

3. Storage

Access tokens are stored locally on the operator's computer. Generated videos are stored locally. No data is sold, shared with third parties or used for advertising. Turing collects no data from other TikTok users or visitors.

4. Retention and deletion

Tokens can be revoked at any time in TikTok under Settings, Security and permissions, Apps and services, and are deleted from the local machine on request.
